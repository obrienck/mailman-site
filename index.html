Mailman

A mobile email client. Your mailbox, your AI, your choice.

Mailman is a mobile email app for iOS and Android built around one idea: you bring your own AI. Most email apps that offer "AI features" route your mail through whichever AI vendor they picked for you. Mailman doesn't — because it doesn't have one of its own.

What Mailman does

  • Connects to your real mailbox. Mailman currently supports Gmail, using Google's own secure sign-in. Your mail is read directly from Google's servers to your device — Mailman has no server of its own in between.
  • Lets you read, organize, and compose mail from a clean mobile interface: view your inbox, open and reply to threads, compose new messages, and move messages you're done with to Trash.
  • Summarizes threads and surfaces action items using an AI provider that you configure and control — either a local model running on your own computer (via Ollama or LM Studio), or a hosted provider you already have an account with (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, or any OpenAI-compatible API), using your own API key or your own local network address.

Email content is only ever sent to two places: your mail provider (Google, to read/send/organize mail you ask it to) and, only if and when you turn on an AI feature, the AI provider you personally configured. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who makes this

Mailman is an independently developed app, built and maintained by a single developer as a personal project. It is not affiliated with Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Ollama, or LM Studio — it simply lets you connect to services you already use.
